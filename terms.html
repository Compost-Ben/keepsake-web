Terms

Using Keepsake

Keepsake is for making keepsakes and sending them to people you choose. You agree to these terms when you first open it, and again whenever they change. You must be 13 or older to use it.

No room for abuse

Keepsake has no tolerance for objectionable content or for abusing anyone. Never use it to:

  • harass, bully, threaten or demean anyone;
  • spread hate against anyone for who they are;
  • share sexual content involving anyone under 18, or intimate images of anyone without their consent;
  • share violence, or urge anyone to harm themselves or others;
  • break the law, or share what is not yours to share;
  • pretend to be someone else, or send spam.

Reporting and blocking

  • Report a keepsake from the menu on its page. It leaves your view at once.
  • Report or block a person from Options on their page, or by holding their face on your Circle. Blocking takes them off your Circle and nothing more from them reaches you; Block, Disconnect & Wipe also removes their keepsakes from your account. It cannot be undone.
  • Every report is read within 24 hours. What breaks these terms is removed for everyone, and the account that sent it may be closed.

What you make

What you make in Keepsake is yours. Keepsake keeps it and delivers it to the people you send it to, as the privacy page says, and nothing more.

Plans

Making keepsakes is free until 1 January 2027; from then, it needs a plan.

A plan lets you make as many keepsakes as you like. There are four: NZ$5 a month or NZ$50 a year; for a family, NZ$20 a month or NZ$200 a year, shared through Apple’s Family Sharing with the payer’s Apple family, up to six people. Elsewhere, the App Store shows your price before you buy.

  • Each plan’s first month is free, once per Apple Account, and the first charge comes when it ends. You can buy a plan before 1 January 2027; its free month starts the day you buy it.
  • A plan renews by itself at the end of each month or year until you cancel.
  • Change or cancel a plan any time with Manage my Subscription, in Keepsake’s Settings. After you cancel, you keep making to the end of the month or year you are in. Deleting your account does not cancel a plan.
  • When a plan ends, making new keepsakes stops until you renew.
  • Keepsake cannot give refunds; Apple can, at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Keepsake can change these terms, plans and prices included, with notice.

Ending

You can delete your account at any time, in Settings. Keepsake can close an account that breaks these terms, without notice.

These terms are from 20 November 2026.
