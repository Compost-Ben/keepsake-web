Something for someone to keep

Coming to the App Store

Make a keepsake from photos, video, sound, a letter or a song, laid out your way. Seal it, and it never changes.

Send it to someone on your Circle, or to someone new with a link. It waits on their sky as a small sun until they open it.

Every keepsake you make or are given stays on your own sky, to arrange and come back to. No ads, no analytics, no trackers.
